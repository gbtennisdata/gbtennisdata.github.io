Notes:
Junior girls a limited to the number of pro events they may play between birthdays.
17 year olds can play 16 tournaments with no AER limits on wild cards.
16 year olds can play 12 tournaments and use 4 wild cards.
15 year olds can play 10 tournaments and use 3 wild cards.
14 year olds can play 8 tournaments and use 3 wild cards. They have further restrictions on the level of tournaments they can play.
1 additional tournament is earned for reaching the final of a junior grand slam.
4 additional tournaments are earned for each remaining year they are a junior if they finish a year in the top 5 of the junior rankings.
Only tournaments played where they earn a WTA ranking point count towards the limits.
Playing in the Grand Slam does count towards the Tournament quota, but playing a Grand Slam with a WC does not count towards the WC quota.
Participation in Billie Jean King Cup do not count towards the limits.
