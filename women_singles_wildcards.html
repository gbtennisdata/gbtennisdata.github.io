Women's singles wild cards 2026

Women are restricted on the number of wild cards they can receive in a calendar year. The WTA only count wild cards the player earned WTA points with towards these totals, but this table shows all wild cards played with. Players that received a wild card but did not play are excluded from this table.

Wild cards allowances are split into "buckets".

  • Grand Slams: Unlimited
  • WTA Tour: 6 wild cards, of which no more than 3 can be main draw wild cards
  • WTA 125: 3 wild cards
  • W100 to W35: 6 wild cards, of which no more than 3 can be main draw wild cards. A "merit" wild card can be earned for each tournament a player wins a match as a main draw wild card. A player can earn up to 3 merit wild cards.
  • W15: As W100 to W35 but counted separately.

    Additional wild cards or exemptions apply to players with long service, champions of majors or WTA finals, or players who reached No 1 in the singles rankings
    16 year olds may only earn ranking points from 4 wild cards between birthdays.
    15 and 14 year olds may only earn ranking points from 3 wild cards between birthdays.
