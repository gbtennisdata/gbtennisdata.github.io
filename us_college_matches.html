ITA Conference Masters and Sectionals

The ITA Conference Masters and Sectional Championships are the last of 3 possible attempts to qualify for the NCAA Individuals Championships in November.

Who will join Mimi Xu, Charlie Robertson and Dylan Davies in singles and Flora Johnson and Ranah Stoiber in doubles as NCAA qualifiers so far?

NCAA qualification:
Singles (Conference Masters): Semi-finalists (4 places)
Singles (Sectionals): Semi-finalists and 2 quarter-final play-off winners in each sectional (6 places in each sectional)
Doubles (Conference Masters): Finalists (2 places)
Doubles (Sectionals): Finalists and 3rd place play-off winner in each sectional (3 places in each sectional)
