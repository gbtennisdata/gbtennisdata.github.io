Tuesday report

On a back court Mimi navigates the first round to win 6-3 6-2. It took her a few games to work out how to play Kyoka Kubo's unothordox style but finally broke to go ahead 4-3 and then proceeded to win 6 straight games. Kubo, who tends to throw in underarm serves, ended Mimi's game streak in the 4th game of the second set.

Ranah Stoiber comes back from a set and a break down to win in 3 sets. After trading breaks for much of the first set, Ranah finally looked to have backed up a break with multiple set points at 5-4 for the first set. Unfortunately, she failed to capitalise and Guichard won that game and the next two to take the first set. She then dropped her opening service game in the second set to go down 0-2, before coming back to take the set 6-4. She took an early 2-0 in the third before being pegged back. She went 5-3 up and served for the match at 5-4 but got broken. She broke the next game to regain the lead, and rapidly took a 40-0 lead in the next game. A nervy final few points ensued before she finally took the match.

Women's favourite Luciana Perry (Ohio State) scraped through 7-5 7-5
The first seed to fall was 9-16 seed Sophia Cisse-Ignatiev (Ohio State) who was defeated by UNC's Maddy Zampardo 6-3 3-6 6-3. Some may regard it as an upset, but I had Zampardo winning this one. In the other half of this 16th was an upset with Auburn's Ashton Bowers being defeated by Rice's Divna Ratkovic.
