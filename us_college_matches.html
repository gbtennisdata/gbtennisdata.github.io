ITA All-American Championships

Women - Cary Tennis Park, Cary, North Carolina
Men - Michael D. Case Tennis Center, University of Tulsa, Oklahoma
19 to 27 September 2026

The ITA All-Americans is the second most prestigious individuals competition in US College Tennis and the first of 3 possible attempts to qualify for the NCAA Individuals Championships in November.

NCAA qualification:
Singles: Quarter-finalists and Consolation draw finalists
Doubles: Semi-finalists and Consolation draw winner
