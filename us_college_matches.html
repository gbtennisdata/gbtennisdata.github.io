How do players qualify for the NCAAs?
Players qualifying by reaching the latter stages of 1 of 4 competitions between September and November.

What is the format of matches?
Singles matches are best of 3 sets. Doubles matches are best of 3 sets with the third set a match tie-break. No let, No ad are played in both singles and doubles.

I've seen players being called All-American. What is that?
All-American honours is the highest honour is US Sports. It means the athlete is a top player in their sport. In tennis, a college athlete is an All-American if they are the All-American Championships winner, reach the round of 16 in the NCAA Individuals Championship or are end the season as a top 20 ranked player. In doubles, they are an All-American if they are the All American Championships winner, reach the quarter-finals in the NCAA Individuals Championships or end the season as a top 10 ranked team.
Mimi Xu, Ranah Stoiber, Luca Pow and Charlie Robertson all earn All-American status in these championships by reaching the round of 16 in singles.

Have any British players won the NCAAs before?
Yes. Paul Jubb (South Carolina) is are only singles winner, winning the title in 2019.
We have plenty of success in doubles including Alice Barnes (2005, Stanford), Cristelle Grier (2006, Northwestern), Dominic Inglot (2009, Virginia), Lloyd Glasspool (2015, Texas) Eden Richardson (2017, Louisiana State), Nell Miller (2022, North Carolina State) and Lui Maxted (2024, TCU). Northern Ireland's Claire Curran (California, Berkeley) also won the doubles title in 2000 but this was before her switch from IRE to GBR.
