ITA Regional Championships

The ITA Regional Championships is the second of 3 possible attempts to qualify for the NCAA Individuals Championships in November.

NCAA qualification:
Singles: Finalists in each region
Doubles: Winner in each region

Below is a list of some of the British student-athletes that may be competing. This list will be updated as entry lists are released.
